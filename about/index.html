About MULAN

MULAN (沐岚) is the brand of Foshan Mulan Zhijia Building Materials Co., Ltd., based in the ceramic headquarters base of Chancheng District, Foshan, Guangdong — one of China's main centres for bathroom and building-material manufacturing.

MULAN designs and supplies aging-friendly bathroom products around one idea: future elderly care, smart living. The range covers seated and split showers, an electric rise-assist stand for the toilet, wall-mounted shower chairs and folding stools, grab bars and armrests, a wheelchair-accessible bathroom cabinet and a complete smart bathroom package.

MULAN sells business to business: to distributors and importers, to OEM brands that want their own aging-friendly line, and to elderly-care operators that furnish care homes and senior apartments. It already ships to the Middle East and West Asia, and Japan is its new focus.
