Wheelchair-accessible smart bathroom cabinet with tilting mirror

Roll under the basin and tilt the mirror to the user's height.

WhatsApp us

In one line

MULAN's smart bathroom cabinet lets a wheelchair roll under the basin, and its mirror tilts by hand from 0 to 15 degrees so users of different heights, seated or standing, can see themselves. It is part of MULAN's aging-friendly smart bathroom package.

Who it's for

  • Wheelchair users and people who wash seated
  • Care homes and senior apartments
  • Distributors completing an accessible bathroom range

Problems it solves

  • Basin cabinets that block a wheelchair
  • Mirrors set for standing adults only
  • Mixing cabinets from a different supplier than the rest of the room

Features

  • Wheelchair accessible

    Space to roll under the basin.

  • Tilting mirror

    Adjust by hand from 0 to 15°.

Frequently asked questions

Can a wheelchair fit under the cabinet?

Yes. The smart bathroom cabinet is listed in MULAN's catalogue as wheelchair accessible, with room for a wheelchair under the basin. Confirm the dimensions for your room with MULAN before ordering.

How does the mirror adjust?

The mirror angle adjusts by hand from 0 to 15 degrees, so it suits users of different heights, including people sitting in a wheelchair.

Can I buy the cabinet on its own?

Ask MULAN on WhatsApp at +86 136 6003 9128. The cabinet is part of the aging-friendly smart bathroom package and is also quoted for projects.
