Wall-mounted folding stool that carries up to 250 kg

Flips down to sit, flips up to clear the floor.

WhatsApp us

In one line

MULAN's wall-mounted folding stool flips 180° up and down, carries up to 250 kg and has raised anti-slip particles on the seat. It is made of ABS and stainless steel with a thickened, anti-static ABS sleeve, and a luminous ring helps users find it in low light.

Who it's for

  • Showers too small for a chair
  • Users who need a quick seat to wash their feet
  • Distributors selling high-load bathroom seating

Problems it solves

  • Seats that cannot take a heavier user
  • Slippery seat surfaces
  • Seats that are hard to find at night

Frequently asked questions

How much weight can the folding stool carry?

The wall-mounted folding stool carries up to 250 kg, according to MULAN's 2025 product manual. It is made of ABS and stainless steel.

Does the stool glow in the dark?

It has a luminous ring that glows after absorbing light, which helps users find the seat in a dim bathroom.

Is the seat slippery when wet?

The seat surface has raised anti-slip particles, and the thickened ABS sleeve is one-piece moulded with rounded edges and is anti-static.

Is it available in different colours?

The catalogue shows the stool in yellow and white. Ask MULAN on WhatsApp at +86 136 6003 9128 for current colours and OEM options.
