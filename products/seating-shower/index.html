Multifunctional seating shower: bathe seated or standing

A freestanding shower with its own seat, so older users can wash sitting down.

WhatsApp us

In one line

MULAN's multifunctional seating shower is a freestanding unit with a seat, so older users or people with limited mobility can shower sitting or standing. Eight adjustable outlets on two shower arms wrap the body in water, with shoulder spray and back massage. It uses at most 9 litres a minute and carries up to 110 kg.

Who it's for

  • People who tire or lose balance when standing to shower
  • Care homes offering independent bathing
  • Distributors adding a seated-bathing product to a safety range

Problems it solves

  • Standing on a wet floor for a whole shower
  • Cold patches when water reaches only part of the body
  • Fixed seats that crowd a small bathroom

Features

  • Two ways to bathe

    Sitting bath or standing bath.

  • 8 adjustable outlets

    Angle each outlet on the two shower arms.

  • Shoulder spray + back massage

    Shoulder water swings up and down.

  • Folding armrests

    Fold away to save space.

  • Water saving

    No more than 9 L/min.

  • Materials

    ABS seat and backrest, stainless steel frame, partial copper.

Specifications

  • 110 kgMaximum load
  • 0.1–0.5 MPaWorking water pressure
  • 133 × 71 × 20 cmOuter box

Source: 2025 product manual.

Frequently asked questions

Can an older person shower sitting down with this unit?

Yes. The seating shower has its own seat, so the user can wash sitting or standing. Eight outlets on two adjustable shower arms surround the body with water, and the shoulder outlets add a swinging spray and back massage. The armrests fold away when not needed.

How much water does the seating shower use?

According to MULAN's 2025 product manual, the water-saving flow is no more than 9 litres per minute at a working water pressure of 0.1 to 0.5 MPa.

What is the maximum load?

The seating shower carries up to 110 kg. The seat and backrest are ABS, the frame is stainless steel with some copper parts, and it ships in a 133 × 71 × 20 cm outer box.
