Electric rise-assist stand: stand up from the toilet at one touch

Lifts the seat quietly at one touch and holds wherever the user stops.

WhatsApp us

In one line

MULAN's electric rise-assist stand fits over the toilet and raises the seat at one touch, silently and to any height, stopping wherever the user releases the button. The front edge rises 130 + 45 mm and tilts 0–13°, helping people with knee problems sit and stand. It carries 135 kg, is rated IPX4 and lasts 20,000 cycles.

Who it's for

  • Older people and people with knee or joint pain
  • Care homes reducing lifting by staff
  • Distributors of mobility and toilet aids

Problems it solves

  • Squatting down to and rising from a low toilet
  • Grab-bar-only setups that still need leg strength
  • Armrests that are slippery when wet

Frequently asked questions

How does the rise-assist stand help at the toilet?

It sits over the toilet and lifts the seat at one touch. The lift is silent, infinitely adjustable and stops wherever the user lets go, so people with knee or joint discomfort can lower themselves and stand up with less effort.

What are the lift range and seat angle?

The front edge rises 130 + 45 mm and the seat plate tilts from 0 to 13 degrees, according to MULAN's 2025 product manual.

Is it safe to use in a wet bathroom?

The stand is rated IPX4 against splashing water. The armrests are antibacterial ABS with curled edges to reduce slipping, and the grip area is silicone for extra friction.

What power supply does it need?

It runs on 100–240 VAC at 50/60 Hz, so it suits most export markets. Standby power is at most 1 W at 230 V and single-channel maximum current is 5 ± 0.5 A.
