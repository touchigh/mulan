Multifunctional split shower with rotating spray arms

Upper and lower spray arms that rotate, with or without a top spray.

WhatsApp us

In one line

MULAN's multifunctional split shower mounts on the wall with upper and lower spray arms that rotate, plus shoulder spray, a hand shower and an optional top spray. It is made of stainless steel and ABS and comes in two versions, with or without the top spray.

Who it's for

  • Bathrooms with room for a wall unit but not a freestanding shower
  • Care operators pairing a shower with a wall-mounted seat
  • Distributors offering two shower formats

Problems it solves

  • A single fixed shower head that misses a seated user
  • No way to direct water to the lower body
  • Projects that need the same shower in rooms of different sizes

Features

  • Rotating arms

    Upper and lower arms both rotate.

  • Four ways to spray

    Spray arm, shoulder spray, showerhead, top spray.

  • Two versions

    With or without top spray.

  • Materials

    Stainless steel + ABS.

Specifications

  • 14 kg / 15 kgGross weight
  • 82 × 70 × 18 cmOuter box

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the two split shower versions?

One version has a top spray and one does not. Both have rotating upper and lower spray arms, shoulder spray and a hand shower. The gross weight is 14 kg or 15 kg depending on the version.

Can the split shower be used with a shower seat?

Yes. Because the lower arm rotates, water can reach a seated user. Many projects pair it with a wall-mounted shower chair or folding stool; ask MULAN which combination suits your room.

What is the split shower made of?

The main materials are stainless steel and ABS. It ships in an 82 × 70 × 18 cm outer box.
