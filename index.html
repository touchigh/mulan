Frequently asked questions

What does MULAN make?

MULAN (Foshan Mulan Zhijia Building Materials Co., Ltd.) makes aging-friendly bathroom products: multifunctional seating and split showers, an electric rise-assist stand for the toilet, wall-mounted shower chairs and folding stools, grab bars and armrests, a wheelchair-accessible smart bathroom cabinet and a complete aging-friendly smart bathroom package.

Who are MULAN products for?

The products are for older adults and people with limited mobility who want to bathe and use the toilet safely and on their own. MULAN sells to businesses: distributors and importers, OEM brands, and elderly-care operators that furnish homes, apartments or care facilities.

Can an older person shower sitting down?

Yes. The multifunctional seating shower has its own seat, so the user can wash seated or standing. Eight adjustable outlets on two shower arms wrap the body in water, with shoulder spray and back massage. It uses no more than 9 litres per minute and carries up to 110 kg.

How does the electric rise-assist stand help at the toilet?

It fits over the toilet and lifts the seat at one touch, silently and to any height, stopping wherever the user releases the button. The front edge rises 130 + 45 mm and the seat tilts 0–13°. It carries 135 kg, is rated IPX4 and lasts 20,000 cycles.

What is in the aging-friendly smart bathroom package?

The package plans a whole bathroom: a human presence sensor, warm aging-friendly lighting, a smart bath heater, anti-slip handrails, an SOS emergency call button, a seated shower, a smart toilet with lift assist, and a smart cabinet that a wheelchair can roll under.

Are MULAN grab bars easy to find at night?

The luminous versions have glowing rings that light up in the dark after absorbing light, so the bar is easier to find at night. The bars also use raised anti-slip particles, and one L-shaped bar has a 36 mm resin grip over an aluminium alloy tube.

Does MULAN offer OEM?

Yes. MULAN offers OEM supply of its aging-friendly bathroom products for brands that want to sell under their own name. Message the team on WhatsApp with your market, the products and expected quantities to discuss specifications and terms.

Which markets does MULAN supply, and how do I get a quote?

MULAN already ships to the Middle East and West Asia and is now expanding to Japan. For B2B pricing, product sheets or OEM requests, message the MULAN team on WhatsApp at +86 136 6003 9128 with the products and quantities you need.
