Questions buyers ask

What does MULAN make?

MULAN (Foshan Mulan Zhijia Building Materials Co., Ltd.) makes aging-friendly bathroom products: multifunctional seating and split showers, an electric rise-assist stand for the toilet, wall-mounted shower chairs and folding stools, grab bars and armrests, a wheelchair-accessible smart bathroom cabinet, an automatic shower machine for care homes, and a complete aging-friendly smart bathroom package.

Who are MULAN products for?

The products are for older adults and people with limited mobility who want to bathe and use the toilet safely and on their own. MULAN sells to businesses: distributors and importers, OEM brands, and elderly-care institutions that furnish homes, apartments or care facilities.

Can an older person shower sitting down?

Yes. The multifunctional seating shower is a standalone unit with a seat, so the user can wash seated or standing. Eight outlets on the two shower arms adjust in angle to wrap the body in water, and it offers shoulder spray and back massage. It uses no more than 9 litres per minute and carries up to 110 kg.

How does the electric rise-assist stand help at the toilet?

It fits over the toilet and lifts the seat at one touch, silently and to any height, stopping wherever the user releases the button. The front edge rises 130 + 45 mm and the seat tilts 0–13°, helping people with knee problems sit down and stand up. It carries 135 kg, is rated IPX4 and lasts 20,000 cycles.

What is in the aging-friendly smart bathroom package?

The package plans a whole bathroom around the idea of future elderly care and smart living. It combines a human presence sensor, warm aging-friendly lighting, a smart bath heater, anti-slip handrails, an SOS emergency call button, a seated shower, a smart toilet with lift assist, and a smart cabinet that a wheelchair can roll under.

Are the grab bars easy to find at night?

The luminous versions of MULAN grab bars have glowing rings that light up in the dark after absorbing light, so the bar is easier to find at night. The bars also use raised anti-slip particles, and the L-shaped bar has a 36 mm resin grip with a built-in aluminium alloy tube.

How does the automatic shower machine help care homes?

A caregiver bathes a resident lying down. A standard wash takes about 4 minutes and about 80–100 litres of water, compared with about 400–600 litres for other bathing equipment. Body and disinfection nozzles run on separate pipes, and water supply stops automatically if the temperature is abnormal.
